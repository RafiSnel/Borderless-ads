# Borderless ad #1 — "Lying"

Brief (from Rafi's answers, 10 Oct 2026)
- Hook: provocation. Later videos, in order: curiosity → relatable pain → big number.
- Look: light cream + terracotta/orange (app palette: bg #f4eee2, accent #a8512b, amber #ee8b47, ink #1f1a14, Inter).
- Tempo: a new visual roughly every 0.7 s, locked to the voice.
- Length: 25–35 s. Clean vertical export (1080x1920), no laptop filming.
- Mascot: a small passport character with eyes that reacts.
- App footage: ~30% (real recordings from the static tour).
- Ending: strong, no hard call to action. Price small on the end card.
- Text: only key words, big, on the beat. Sound: SFX on every cut + soft beat.
- Message: everyone else gets the same lesson; Borderless builds a course around you.
- Tone: cheeky but smart. No competitor named or drawn.
- Voice: Kirk (British), fastest setting, pauses tightened.
