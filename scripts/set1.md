## 07 · "Top 5 hardest languages" — TAALFUN · vibe DONKER NEON + wereldkaart
Beeld: neon wereldkaart, per taal licht een gebied op, neon-cijfer aftellen 5→1, korte "why"-iconen.
1. The five hardest languages for English speakers, according to the US diplomats' own school.
2. Five: Arabic. Written right to left, and letters change shape depending on where they sit.
3. Four: Korean. The alphabet is easy. The politeness levels are not.
4. Three: Cantonese. Six tones. Say a word a little higher, and it means something else.
5. Two: Mandarin. Four tones, and thousands of characters to read.
6. One: Japanese. Three writing systems, and you speak differently depending on who you're talking to.
7. Each one: around two thousand two hundred class hours.
8. Hard? Yes. Impossible? No.
9. All five are on Borderless.

## 08 · "Top 5 easiest languages" — TAALFUN · vibe POP + wereldkaart
Beeld: felle kleurvlakken, platte kaart met grote stickers, getallen die stuiteren.
1. The five easiest languages for English speakers. Number one is closer than you think.
2. Five: Italian. You read it exactly how it's written.
3. Four: Spanish. Same trick, plus words you already know. Hospital. Chocolate. Idea.
4. Three: Swedish. Verbs don't change for I, you or they.
5. Two: Norwegian. Same deal, and the word order feels almost English.
6. One: Dutch. One of English's closest relatives. Water. Warm. Appel.
7. Around six hundred class hours each. That's twenty-four weeks.
8. So… what's your excuse?
9. Borderless. Fifty lessons, built around why you're learning.
